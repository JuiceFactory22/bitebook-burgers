Questions, answered

Is this actually free food, or a gimmick?

Your $15 covers roughly what one burger costs. Every other coupon in your passport — from every other participating restaurant — gets you a free burger with any other purchase. It's real, and it's how restaurants can afford to take part.

When do I get my passport?

Your digital passport is emailed on October 1, when Burger Blastoff officially kicks off. That's also when the full restaurant lineup is locked in, so your passport reflects everyone participating.

How do I redeem a coupon?

Show your digital passport (from the email) to your server at any participating restaurant, along with your other purchase. They'll mark that coupon as used — one redemption per restaurant per passport.

How many restaurants will be in the passport?

We're finalizing the Phoenix lineup now — expect somewhere between 5 and 10+ restaurants. Either way, your $15 covers the first one; the rest are free.

What's your refund policy?

Since spots are limited and your passport is reserved the moment you purchase, all sales are final. You'll receive your digital passport by email on October 1 — reach out if it doesn't arrive.
