Wing Month Is Coming to Greater Nashville: The First Deals

From December 15 to January 15, one BiteBook passport gets you a wing deal at local spots across the Greater Nashville area. Restaurants are still signing up, but here's who's in so far.

More restaurants are joining every week. Anyone added after you buy shows up in your passport automatically, so there's no need to wait for the full list.

How the passport works

  1. Get your passport. It's $20, and you can buy a few if you're bringing friends. Each passport has its own set of coupons.
  2. It lands in your inbox. You get a confirmation right away, and the passport itself arrives by email when Wing Month kicks off on December 15.
  3. Go eat. Tell your server you have a BiteBook coupon and show the QR code on your phone. They scan it and you get the deal. Each coupon works once.

A couple of good-guest notes: add-ons, upgrades and tax are extra, and please tip on the full value of your meal. Great guests are what keep local spots saying yes to deals like these.

Why we do this

BiteBook exists to send hungry locals to independent restaurants they haven't tried yet. Restaurants don't pay anything to be in the passport; they offer one deal, and we handle the promotion. If you're not happy with your passport for any reason, email us and we'll refund you in full.
